Privacy

TML Unofficial is a free, unofficial fan site for the Tomorrowland Thailand 2026 line-up. It is not run by or connected to Tomorrowland.

What we store

  • Your username, display name and a colour for your avatar.
  • If you sign in with Google: a Google account ID so we can recognise you next time, and your name as a suggested display name. We don't ask for or store your email address.
  • If you sign up with a password: a salted hash of it, never the password itself.
  • The sets you save, and the chat messages you send.

What other people see

Your display name, avatar and the sets you save appear in "who's going" lists. Chat messages are visible to everyone in that room.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, and no selling or sharing your data. A session cookie keeps you signed in.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything linked to it, write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
